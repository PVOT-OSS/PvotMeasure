Measure is a measuring tape and a spirit level in your pocket, with a clean design and nothing in the way.

Measure in the room
• Point your camera at a surface and tap Add point to measure from one spot to the next
• Chain points to measure along a wall or around a table, with each segment and the total shown
• Undo the last point, finish a line and start another, or clear them all
• Uses depth on supported phones to place points on any surface, not only flat ones
• Lengths in centimetres and metres, or feet and inches

Level anything
• Lay your phone flat: two glass spheres drift apart as it tilts and merge into one bubble at level
• Stand it upright: a horizon stays level with the world as you turn it
• The tilt in degrees, a screen that floods with colour and a gentle tap when you hit 0°

Made for your phone
• Dark and light themes
• Liquid glass on the navigation bar, or a lighter frosted look if you prefer
• Built on Pvot, the design system behind PVOT-OSS's apps

Private and open
• No ads, no accounts, no tracking
• The camera is used only live, to measure; nothing is recorded or saved
• Free and open source under the GPL-3.0-or-later licence: github.com/PVOT-OSS/PvotMeasure

Measuring in the room needs a phone that supports Google Play Services for AR. Measurements are estimates: move slowly and keep surfaces well lit for the best results. The level works on every phone.
